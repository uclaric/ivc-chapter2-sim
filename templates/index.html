HOW TO USE THE SIM

🔊 LISTENING TO THE DISCUSSION

Turn Character Audio ON if you want to hear the characters speak. Use Replay Last to hear the most recent speaker again. Use Pause Audio to stop the audio.

🎧 FOLLOW THE CONVERSATION

Each speaker appears automatically and the transcript scrolls with the conversation. With Character Audio ON, the next speaker appears when the current voice finishes. Use Play/Pause, −10 sec, +10 sec, or Replay Speaker whenever you need more control.

⌨️ TYPE YOUR RESPONSE

When the group asks you a question, type your answer in the response box. Select Continue or press Enter when ready. Use Shift + Enter for a new line.

🎤 SPEAK YOUR RESPONSE

Select Speak My Response, say your answer, then stop the recording. Your words will appear in the response box. Review and correct the transcription before sending it.

📋 SUBMITTING YOUR WORK TO CANVAS

1) Select Copy Full Transcript.

2) Return to the Chapter 2 Sim-Discussion assignment in Canvas.

3) Choose Text Entry.

4) Paste the complete transcript into the Text Entry box.

5) Review the pasted transcript.

6) Select Submit Assignment.

7) Confirm that Canvas shows the submission as successfully submitted.

Your Sim Transcript

Your conversation is saved as you go. You can copy it here any time, and the final copy prompt will appear again when the Sim ends.

Sim Help

This help is for operating the Sim. It does not provide answers to graded discussion questions.

  • Microphone: choose Speak my response, allow microphone access, talk, then choose Stop & transcribe.
  • Review first: your spoken words appear in the response box. Edit anything the transcription got wrong before sending.
  • Pacing: each speaker appears automatically. The transcript scrolls to the active speaker and stops when it is your turn to respond.
  • Audio: turn Character Audio on or off at any time. Use Play/Pause, rewind 10 seconds, fast forward 10 seconds, or Replay Speaker. Written dialogue always remains available.
  • Connection: if a request fails, your typed response remains in the box so you can try again.
  • Refresh: the visible transcript is restored after an accidental refresh when the browser session is still available.
